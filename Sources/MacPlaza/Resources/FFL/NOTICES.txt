The source distribution includes the app adapter and dependency sources in ThirdParty/.
Nintendo FFL/AFL resource archives are not included. Imported resources stay on this Mac.

Original menu asset adapter (Mac Plaza 0.6)
The SARC/Yaz0/BFRES data reader and original animation player are Mac Plaza code.
Binary format field references: https://github.com/KillzXGaming/BfresLibrary
The modified FFL WASM includes ninTexUtils BC3 decompression and a bounded menu-atlas conversion entry point. See the corresponding ThirdParty sources.
Nintendo menu model, texture, animation and FFL resource data are imported separately, remain in local Application Support, and are not included in this app or its corresponding-source archive. The application never downloads assets or executes Wii U firmware.
