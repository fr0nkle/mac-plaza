# Mac Plaza 0.21.0 — local-first Discord and a livelier plaza

This Mac-only testing update gathers the October 14 work into one release.

- Sign in directly to Discord on your Mac. Friends and availability load without the Plaza service, including after restarting the app. Authorization uses the official Social SDK and PKCE; no Discord client secret or bot token is installed on your device.
- Keep encrypted discussion snapshots and pending likes, replies, profile changes and activity read state on this Mac. Read saved discussions while offline; queued changes retry after reconnecting with stable IDs to prevent duplicate replies.
- Use one Miiverse browser for public and private posts, communities, search, drawings, replies and likes. New posts default to Public for your authorized Plaza friends; existing private posts and private drafts stay private. Notes, replies, likes and activity expire after seven days.
- See one to three speech bubbles at a time, anchored to different Miis, with reading pauses and overlap prevention.
- Miis explore the visible floor and visit other app gatherings. Left/right edge routes wrap around the plaza. The floor extends to the screen edges, including wallpaper margins and zoomed-out views.
- Your primary Mii spends time near the center, makes occasional outings, and welcomes visitors. Guests wave hello, chat, exchange goodbye waves and return to their groups; your Mii answers their waves.
- Short notification chimes no longer interrupt plaza music. Sustained playback and calls still pause it. Music transitions, custom music validation and recovery checks are retained.
- Includes today's resource-pack import, public Discord identity, diagnostics, UI sound and automatic home-screen work, with the latest fixes and regression coverage.

The live Discord check loaded all 226 friends with an unreachable Plaza-service address, including after a cold start. The original service address was restored afterward.

Shared note delivery, bot custom statuses and in-app updates still use the shared service. Container deployment files are included for moving that service off the developer's Mac; that migration has not been deployed. This release does not introduce peer-to-peer bot hosting.

Requires Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later. This preview is ad-hoc signed and is **not Apple notarized**. Public app/source bundles omit optional Nintendo recordings and console resources; local imports and synthesized audio remain available. Windows distribution remains retired. Source and checksums accompany the installer.
