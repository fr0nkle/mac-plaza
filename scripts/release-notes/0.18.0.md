Miiverse now has a button above Write Note and opens in a floating window over the moving wallpaper. Browse All Notes, My Notes, Friends’ Notes, The Plaza and individual app communities. Communities use Steam App IDs or app bundle IDs, include authorized friend notes for apps absent from your home screen, and preserve private notes on this Mac. Search includes text, author, drawing descriptions and community names; sort by newest, oldest or most liked. Drawings, shared likes/replies, local note editing and deletion undo use the existing journal controls. Browsing leaves saved notes, drafts, apps and Miis unchanged.

Miiverse has a separate offline soundtrack with an in-window volume slider and Settings → Music import, preview and volume controls. Writing a note or reply crossfades to writing music and returns to Miiverse afterwards. The supplied local Miiverse recording is excluded from distributable app and source archives.
