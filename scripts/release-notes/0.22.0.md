Public Miiverse posts can now be seen, liked and replied to by everyone signed in with Discord, without requiring a friendship. Posts and replies use the verified Discord display name and primary Mii.

New posts default to Public. Friends Only and Private remain available. Existing shared posts and drafts stay friends-only; private posts stay private. A Public Posts section makes community posts easy to find, while Friends’ Notes remains limited to friends. Authors can be blocked from the post, and blocks also prevent reads and interactions.

The bottom-left Report a Bug button opens an email to alexanderhosier@squidproductions.org with the app version and a blank report template. Nothing is sent automatically.

Saved Miis, notes, drafts and settings are preserved. The existing seven-day post retention and offline delivery queue remain in place. Public Miiverse delivery requires the updated Plaza service, which is deployed with this release.

Mac: Apple silicon, macOS 15 or later. This testing preview is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple notarized; Developer ID distribution setup is deferred.
