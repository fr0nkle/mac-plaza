Notes and Miiverse posts now expire automatically seven days after their original posting time. Their replies, likes and associated activity are removed with them. Editing a post does not extend its lifetime.

The note composer, journal and Miiverse show the retention notice. Drafts, saved Miis, apps and preferences are preserved. Cleanup runs on startup and every minute, including offline local notes; the sharing service also rejects expired cached uploads and cleans up while idle.
